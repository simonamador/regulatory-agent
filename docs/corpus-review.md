## Requirements

| Id | Text (summary) | Formal reading | Approved |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-101 | Glucose ≤ 55 → Urgent Low alert within 5 min, any mode | ANY · urgent_low · within 300 s inclusive · urgent_low_alert | yes |
| REQ-102 | No glucose alerts in the first 30 min (warm-up) | WARMUP [0,1800) · always · ¬urgent_low_alert | yes |
| REQ-103 | No glucose values during warm-up | WARMUP · always · ¬glucose_shown | yes |
| REQ-104 | While active, transmit a reading at least every 5 min — 21 CFR 862.1355(b)(2) | ACTIVE · always triggered · within 300 s exclusive · reading_sent | yes |
| REQ-105 | No data for 10 min → Signal Loss alert within 1 min — (b)(1)(vii) | ACTIVE · data_gap · within 60 s inclusive · signal_loss_alert | yes |
| REQ-106 | Expired sensor → no glucose display — (b)(5) | EXPIRED · always · ¬glucose_shown | yes |
| REQ-107 | Urgent low with an expired sensor → alert (vacuity target) | EXPIRED · urgent_low · within 300 s · urgent_low_alert | yes |
| REQ-108 | Labeling describes the alert rates — (b)(7)(iv) | **not formalized**: stays `pending` | left pending |

Modeling decisions:
- The 5 min, 10 min and 1 min thresholds are ours; the regulation does not set them. Each `source.note` says so.
- REQ-101 is anchored in the FreeStyle Libre 3 510(k) decision summary: the Urgent Low alert is mandatory and cannot be turned off.
- **The G01 conflict is realistic.** Real CGMs give no readings during warm-up and resolve it through labeling ("use your blood glucose meter"). Snapshot v2 shows exactly that correction: scoping REQ-101 to `ACTIVE`.
- AX-101: warm-up lasts exactly 30 min. AX-102: an expired sensor measures no glucose.
